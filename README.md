> **LoopGrid** bridges the gap between Indian manufacturing MSMEs (Resource Enterprises) generating industrial offcuts/scrap and secondary recycling foundries (Processing Enterprises), providing standardized pooling, transparent per-kilogram stock-exchange pricing, integrated green working capital, and automated statutory compliance.

---

## 🌟 Key Platform Capabilities

### 1. 📈 Live Prices Secondary Stock Exchange
* **Spot Benchmarks Quoted Strictly in ₹/kg**: Real-time ticker for industrial commodities (Aluminum 6061 Swarf, Copper Heavy Scrap, Brass Honey Scrap, MS Scrap, Polypropylene Flakes, HDPE Regrind).
* **Dynamic Market Fluctuations**: Simulates order book depth, supply liquidity, secondary demand pressure, and NABL certified spectrographic purity indices.
* **Price Calculation Explainer**: Visual interactive guide breaking down scrap valuations based on:
  * Primary Virgin Base Metal Prices (LME / MCX Spot)
  * Secondary Smelting Yield & Thermal Re-melt Loss
  * NABL Chemical Assay & Metallurgical Purity Grade
  * Freight Distance & CPCB Hazardous Logistics Handling

### 2. 🛒 Marketplace & Integrated Credit Facility
* **Standardized Lot Procurement**: Browse categorized, NABL-assayed scrap lots ready for secondary melting and remanufacturing.
* **Embedded Working Capital Line**:
  * Partnered with SIDBI, Tata Capital, and invoice financing lenders.
  * Instant credit approval up to ₹50 Lakhs with 15 to 90-day repayment tenures.
  * DRP Passport escrow holding until delivery acceptance and CPCB Schedule-II manifest handshake.
* **How Marketplace Works Visual Flow**: Step-by-step breakdown from lot reservation, credit sanction, managed carrier dispatch, to dockside purity validation.

### 3. 🛡️ Statutory Compliance & Green MSME Hub
* **Statutory Compliance Engine**:
  * Direct synchronization with **GSTN** (GSTR-3B scrap reconciliations), **EPFO** (ECR generation), **State Factories & Boilers Dept** (annual license renewals), **CPCB** (Form-10 Hazardous Scrap movement manifests), and **SPCB** (Air & Water Consent to Operate - CTO).
  * **1-Click File**: Simulates instant e-filing with automated Acknowledgement Reference Number (ARN) receipt issuance and compliance score boosts.
* **100-Point Assessment Checklist & Frugal Hacks**:
  * 14 real-world CPCB/SPCB/BEE regulations.
  * Actionable operational efficiency tips (e.g. why color-coded machine segregation boosts scrap value by 22%).
  * Low-cost frugal MSME implementation hacks (modular second-hand drip trays, 3-chamber baffle drum separators, refurbished HDPE drum sorting).
* **Enterprise Green Compliance Dossier**:
  * Tamper-evident printable digital compliance certificate with QR verification code, verified Udyam ID, ZED Silver rating, and SHA-256 digital ledger integrity hash.

### 4. 🏅 MSME Sustainable (ZED) Certification Desk
* **Ministry of MSME & Quality Council of India (QCI) Initiative**:
  * **80% Subsidy** for Micro Enterprises
  * **60% Subsidy** for Small Enterprises
  * **50% Subsidy** for Medium Enterprises
  * Additional **10% grant** for Women-owned, SC/ST, and North East Region / Himalayan units.
  * Up to **₹5,00,000 Handholding Consulting Support** from QCI.
  * **0.5% Bank Interest Rate Concession** across public sector banks (SBI, PNB, Canara, BoB).
  * **Green Channel SPCB Exemption** from routine inspection audits.
* **Certification Progression**: Bronze (5 params) ➔ Silver (14 params - verified) ➔ Gold (20 params) ➔ Diamond (30 params).
* **Upgrade to Gold Desk**: Direct in-app application pre-populated with Udyam KYC and DRP transaction records.

### 5. ⚡ Green Capital & Credit Simulator
* **Interactive Financing Simulator**:
  * Sliders for Capital Amount (₹5 Lakhs to ₹5 Crores) and Repayment Tenure (1 to 10 Years).
  * Purpose selectors for Rooftop Solar PV, Effluent Treatment Plants (ETP/ZLD), Clean Electric Furnaces, and High-Efficiency VFD Machinery.
  * **Live Financial Comparison**: Displays monthly EMI at 7.5% subsidized concessional rate vs. 11.2% commercial benchmark, detailing total interest saved in ₹ Lakhs.
* **Direct SIDBI Application**: Pre-qualification dossier dispatched directly to SIDBI Single Window Nodal Officers.

### 6. 🏛️ Government Schemes Directory
* **SIDBI 4E (End-to-End Energy Efficiency)**: Up to ₹500L, 90% project cost covered, 7.00%–7.50% interest rate.
* **SIDBI SPEED**: 100% solar financing up to ₹100L with zero collateral and 48-hour automated sanction.
* **SIDBI STAR**: Rooftop solar & cleaner fuels with 2% interest subvention.
* **CGTMSE Green Credit Guarantee Scheme**: 100% sovereign credit guarantee cover up to ₹5 Crores without demanding third-party collateral or personal property mortgages.
* **CLCSS (Credit Linked Capital Subsidy)**: 15% upfront cash grant up to ₹15 Lakhs for eco-tech upgrades.
* **PRSF (Partial Risk Sharing Facility)**: World Bank / SIDBI 75% loan loss risk cover for energy-saving projects.
* **CPCB EPR Circular Trading**: Direct monetization of secondary recycling credits.

### 7. 📄 Digital Resource Passports (DRP) & Transit Tracking
* Immutable digital passports for every scrap batch specifying origin, metallurgical composition, carbon abatement metrics, and chain of custody.
* Managed transit tracker with live GPS status, carrier assignment, and driver manifests complying with CPCB Hazardous Waste Movement Rules.

### 8. 📊 Waste Diversion Over Time Analytics
* Interactive Recharts visualization tracking monthly and cumulative diversion of industrial waste from municipal landfills.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Prerequisites
* Node.js (v18.0.0 or higher)
* npm or bun

### Installation
```bash
# Clone repository
git clone https://github.com/darshil0728-create/PlatformLoopGrid-.git
cd PlatformLoopGrid-

# Install dependencies
npm install

# Start development server
npm run dev
```

Visit `http://localhost:3000` in your browser.

### Build & Production
```bash
# Type check and build bundle
npm run build

# Preview production build
npm run preview
```
